# Intention Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 11, 2026_

Intention helps you use your phone on purpose. It is built to work entirely on your iPhone.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Intention has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We don't
operate servers that receive your data.

## What stays on your iPhone

- The intentions you write, your reminder and time-limit choices, and your recent history.
- Your settings, such as default reminder interval and haptics.

This information is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it. You can also clear
your history at any time in **Settings → Clear History**.

## Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. They are not sent through any
server.

## Microphone and speech recognition

If you tap the microphone to say your intention, Intention uses Apple's Speech framework to turn
your words into text. Recognition runs on your iPhone when your device supports it. If it doesn't,
iOS may send the audio to Apple to transcribe it, under
[Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Intention never stores or shares
your audio.

## Children

Intention is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
